  <section>
    <h2 class="text-xl font-semibold mb-3">Task</h2>

    <p class="leading-relaxed text-base-content/80 mb-4">
      Build an interactive Framer prototype of the initial screens for a unified dashboard, consolidating Slack messages, email threads, and video review workflows into a single interface for agency and movie studio admins.
    </p>

    <p class="leading-relaxed text-base-content/80">
      Along the way, I identified and resolved several issues in the initial design, improving hierarchy, navigation flow, and overall usability before delivering the final prototype.
    </p>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2 class="text-xl font-semibold mb-3">Process</h2>
    <p class="leading-relaxed text-base-content/80 mb-4">
      The work started from an initial Figma design. Where interactions and flow weren't specified, I asked the right questions to fill in the gaps before building, avoiding assumptions that would have required rework later.
    </p>
    <p class="leading-relaxed text-base-content/80">
      Complex interactions were handled through Framer code overrides, including passing state via URL parameters to drive nested component changes on click events, keeping the prototype accurate to how the real app would behave.
    </p>
  </section>
